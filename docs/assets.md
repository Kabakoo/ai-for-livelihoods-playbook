# Assets, provenance, and reuse

This repository includes the images and drawings from [Kabakoo Academies’](https://www.kabakoo.africa/) [web edition 7](https://playbook.kabakoo.africa/versions.html).

## Documentary images and interface examples

Photographs and learner likenesses are not covered by the playbook’s general text license. Contact [Kabakoo](mailto:akwaba@kabakoo.africa) before reusing them in another publication. Retain the original captions and context; do not infer additional identities or present a documentary image as evidence of an unrelated event. Screenshots and third-party interface elements retain their separate rights; inclusion here does not grant ownership of the depicted interfaces or brands.
