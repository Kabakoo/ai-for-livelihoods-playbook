# Building AI Applications for Livelihoods

**Lessons and tools from [Kabakoo Academies](https://www.kabakoo.africa/), drawn from its WhatsApp learning system.**

**[Read the playbook](https://playbook.kabakoo.africa/)** · [Use the working tools](https://playbook.kabakoo.africa/resources.html)

How can an AI-supported learning system help people develop their livelihoods? This playbook shares practical lessons from Kabakoo’s work: framing a useful problem, designing learning experiences, building AI mentors, supporting participation, and learning from experiments.

It is written for organizations building or adapting AI-supported learning tools. Ten chapters combine practical guidance with learner stories, interface examples, explanatory drawings, and ten working tools to apply to your own project.

## Explore the playbook

- [Start with the chapter guide](https://playbook.kabakoo.africa/): find the decisions most relevant to your work.
- [Use the working tools](https://playbook.kabakoo.africa/resources.html): develop a problem brief, map assumptions, and plan your next steps.
- [Explore the field notes](https://playbook.kabakoo.africa/field-notes.html): read learner stories and examine examples from the learning system.
- [Consult the sources](https://playbook.kabakoo.africa/sources.html): understand the evidence behind the guidance and its limitations.

Web edition 7 was published on September 14, 2026. [Five further chapters](https://playbook.kabakoo.africa/coming-next.html) will examine outcomes, governance, teams, costs, and adaptation.

## About Kabakoo

[Kabakoo Academies](https://www.kabakoo.africa/) builds learning experiences and tools that support livelihoods. This playbook draws on the work of its learners, team, and collaborators. Read the [collective acknowledgments](https://playbook.kabakoo.africa/versions.html).
